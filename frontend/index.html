DIGITAL DARK SPOT

Find What's Missing. Verify What Matters.

An AI-powered research intelligence platform that discovers information gaps, compares conflicting claims and identifies outdated information across multiple sources.

Start Research Explore Features

How it works

  1. Add up to six source URLs or paste text
  2. Pages are fetched safely and cleaned
  3. Gemini extracts claims with verbatim evidence
  4. Gaps, conflicts and stale dates are flagged
  5. Work through a checklist, export a PDF

Core features

Gap detection

Shows what the reviewed sources do not answer, never claiming the information does not exist.

Conflict analysis

Puts differing claims side by side, accounting for dates and context.

Freshness rules

Category-specific limits: a scholarship deadline ages faster than a science article.

Evidence mapping

Every finding links to its source and to text actually found there.

Coverage score

Share of research questions with sufficient evidence. Not a truth score.

Verification checklist

Tasks generated from gaps and conflicts, saved in PostgreSQL.

Why Digital Dark Spot?

Most AI tools give a confident answer. This one shows where the answer is thin, so you know what to verify.

Technology

Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Gemini, PDFKit, Chart.js, vanilla JavaScript. Built for SHINCHAN ONLINE HACKATHON 2026.
